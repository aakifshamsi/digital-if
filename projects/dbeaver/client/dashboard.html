🔗 Connect Your Domain
Point your existing domain to your new website. Three easy ways:
1️⃣ CNAME Method (Easiest) ▼

1. Log in to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)

2. Find DNS settings → add or edit CNAME record:

Host: www
Points to: dh-beaver.pages.dev

3. Wait 1–2 hours for DNS to update

4. Click the button below to let us know

2️⃣ Nameserver Method (Full Control) ▼

1. Log in to your domain registrar

2. Find Nameserver settings and point to Cloudflare:

nora.ns.cloudflare.com
porter.ns.cloudflare.com

3. Tell us your domain — we'll manage DNS from there

✅ Gives us full control over your domain's DNS

3️⃣ Domain Transfer (We Manage It) ▼

1. Request an EPP code / auth code from your current registrar

2. Tell us the EPP code and domain name

3. We transfer the domain to our registrar

✅ We handle all DNS and domain management
